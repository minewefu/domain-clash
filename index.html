Made of code

  • No image files, no audio samples, no voices. Every pixel is drawn by JavaScript on a 640×360 canvas, and every note, hit and gust of wind is synthesized with the Web Audio API.
  • One palette. Each frame is snapped to Kerrie Lake's 64-colour Resurrect 64 with two-colour ordered dithering after Joel Yliluoma.
  • A fight written as data. A skeletal rig, a choreography language with fighting-game frame data (startup, active, recovery, hit-stop), a street-level 3D Shinjuku, Voxel Space flyovers, and a damage ledger that keeps every crater and severed tower for the rest of the film.

Fan work

An unofficial, non-commercial tribute. Jujutsu Kaisen © Gege Akutami / Shueisha. The characters are original pixel interpretations; no manga panels were traced and no dialogue is used.
